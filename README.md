# Bayyinah · الْبَيِّنَة

**A bilingual (English & Tamil) Quran study assistant** based on *Dr. Rashad Khalifa's English translation* — "Quran: The Final Testament."

> **God Alone · Quran Alone**

Bayyinah lets you ask questions about the Quran in English or Tamil and receive answers grounded in the text, with verse citations. It is built to explain, clarify, and offer reflection rooted in the scripture itself.

🌐 **Live:** [bayyinah-chi.vercel.app](https://bayyinah-chi.vercel.app)

---

## Features

- **Bilingual** — ask and receive answers in English or Tamil.
- **Grounded answers** — every response is drawn from the Quran's verses, with citations (sura:verse), rather than outside opinion.
- **Word & letter counts** — reports the documented figures from the mathematical research (e.g. the word "God" occurs 2698 times, 19×142), alongside transparent computed counts where applicable.
- **The number 19** — references the 19-based mathematical patterns described in Dr. Khalifa's appendices.
- **Courteous and warm** — greets visitors with proper adab and offers gentle, scripture-based guidance.

## A note on this project

Bayyinah presents the Quran through the lens of Dr. Rashad Khalifa's translation and the associated Submitters' perspective ("God Alone, Quran Alone"). This is a specific tradition; readers from other backgrounds may hold different views. Bayyinah is intended as a **study aid for reflection** — not a replacement for reading the Quran directly, nor for the guidance of knowledgeable people.
